# Research Methodology: Time-Aware Semantic News Intelligence

## 1. Problem Formulation and Research Questions

The core objective of News Event Detection and Tracking (NEDT) is to cluster news stories describing the same real-world event across multiple sources and publication timestamps, while distinguishing distinct events that share common entities, locations, or topics.

### Primary Research Question
> **Does combining semantic similarity with temporal proximity and named-entity overlap improve news event clustering compared with conventional TF-IDF and semantic-embedding baselines?**

### Hypotheses
1. **$H_1$ (Temporal Disambiguation)**: Incorporating an exponential temporal decay prevents merging distinct events that involve identical entities but occur weeks or months apart.
2. **$H_2$ (Entity Grounding)**: Jaccard overlap of extracted named entities (persons, organizations, geopolitical locations) improves precision by filtering semantically similar but entity-disjoint stories.
3. **$H_3$ (Representation Granularity)**: Dense sentence transformer embeddings (`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`) surpass bag-of-words TF-IDF representations in capturing lexical paraphrasing across competing news agencies.

---

## 2. Mathematical Model Formulation

### Component 1: Dense Semantic Cosine Similarity ($S_{\text{semantic}}$)
Given text representations $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^d$ generated by SentenceTransformer `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`:
$$S_{\text{semantic}}(A, B) = \frac{u \cdot v}{\|u\|_2 \|v\|_2}$$
To calibrate cosine similarity to a strictly positive range $[0.0, 1.0]$:
$$S_{\text{semantic}}^{\text{norm}} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{S_{\text{semantic}} + 1.0}{2.0}, 0.0, 1.0\right)$$

### Component 2: Exponential Temporal Decay ($S_{\text{temporal}}$)
Given publication timestamps $t_A, t_B$:
$$\Delta t = \frac{|t_A - t_B|_{\text{seconds}}}{3600.0} \quad (\text{hours})$$
$$S_{\text{temporal}}(A, B) = \exp\left( -\frac{\Delta t}{\tau} \right)$$
Where $\tau$ is the half-decay window (default: $\tau = 72.0\text{ hours}$). If either article has a missing timestamp, a neutral uninformative prior of $S_{\text{temporal}} = 0.5$ is assigned.

### Component 3: Named-Entity Jaccard Overlap ($S_{\text{entity}}$)
Given entity sets $E_A, E_B$ extracted via NER:
$$S_{\text{entity}}(A, B) = \begin{cases}
\frac{|E_A \cap E_B|}{|E_A \cup E_B|} & \text{if } |E_A \cup E_B| > 0 \\
0.5 & \text{otherwise (neutral prior)}
\end{cases}$$

### Component 4: Unified Hybrid Formulation ($S_{\text{combined}}$)
$$S_{\text{combined}}(A, B) = \alpha \cdot S_{\text{semantic}}(A, B) + \beta \cdot S_{\text{temporal}}(A, B) + \gamma \cdot S_{\text{entity}}(A, B)$$
Subject to the convex simplex constraint:
$$\alpha + \beta + \gamma = 1.0, \quad \alpha \ge 0, \beta \ge 0, \gamma \ge 0$$

---

## 3. Clustering Architecture

The pairwise distance between articles $A$ and $B$ is defined as:
$$D(A, B) = 1.0 - S_{\text{combined}}(A, B)$$
Distance-thresholded Agglomerative Clustering is applied with average linkage:
$$\text{dist\_threshold} = 1.0 - \theta_{\text{sim}}$$
Where $\theta_{\text{sim}} \in [0.55, 0.70]$ is the calibrated clustering similarity threshold.

### Noise Policy
Clusters with fewer than $\text{min\_cluster\_size} = 2$ members are classified as singleton noise ($C_i = -1$) to prevent forcing unrelated outlier stories into artificial clusters.
